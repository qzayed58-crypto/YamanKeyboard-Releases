Indonesian optional language-pack data attribution

This optional pack contains 70,383 Indonesian base and headword entries and 81,031 unique suggestion entries. The lists contain only individual lexical words and attested word forms; definitions, example sentences, quotations, and source passages are not redistributed. The pack is downloaded only when the user requests Indonesian in keyboard settings.

Indonesian verbs do not inflect for grammatical person or tense. The list therefore does not invent person/tense paradigms; it combines frequency-ranked forms with forms attested in the extracted word data, including active/passive, imperative, reduplicated, and derived forms where available. Nonstandard, slang, dialectal, archaic, and obsolete entries are filtered where marked by the source. The project’s local whole-word exclusion rules are applied before the lists are written. No runtime morphology engine is shipped; the app reads verified flat word lists.
