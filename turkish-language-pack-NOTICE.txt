Turkish optional language-pack data attribution

This optional pack contains 84,657 lemma entries and 420,318 unique suggestion entries. The two word lists total 5,982,853 uncompressed bytes. They contain lexical entries only, not source passages. The words are assembled from the pinned Turkish Wordfreq list, vetted general lexical roots from TRmorph, curated country/capital/city/landmark/waterway/specialist entries, GeoNames city-name components, and offline-generated inflections. Blocklisted complete words and their specified ة/ه spelling variants are excluded by the local build filter.

Morphology is precomputed offline; no Foma, TRmorph engine, runtime morphology, or new suggestion-generation algorithm is included. All accepted noun roots receive plural, core case, and third-person possessive forms; broader possessive and derivational forms are limited to smaller frequency-ranked subsets. The common affirmative and negative person forms for five tenses are generated for accepted verbs. All accepted verbs also receive ability/inability forms in the past, aorist, and future, with continuous and conditional ability plus evidential, necessitative, imperfective, and copula forms prioritized for more frequent verbs. Adjectives receive useful nominalized forms, and case suffixes are generated only for curated place-name roots rather than every GeoNames component.

Curated spelling and fact references include the UN M49 list, UN world-capital names, NGA Geographic Names Server, Natural Earth, UNESCO World Heritage List, Google Machine Learning Glossary, and Türkiye MFA country/capital list; only lexical names and components are retained.

The pack is not included in the base app and is downloaded only when the user requests the optional Turkish language. The user may remove the downloaded language pack and its local files from the app settings.
