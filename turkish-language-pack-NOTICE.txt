Turkish language data attribution

This optional language pack contains a Turkish word-frequency vocabulary derived from the Turkish list distributed by wordfreq:
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
Source revision: 912caf64b657478d1dff1138efdc078947d54bb1
The source list was filtered with Turkish morphological analysis to retain attested forms associated with 10,000 frequent distinct lemmas.
